What we do

Four ways to work with TTI.

01

Curriculum & Instructional Design

Build learning experiences around the needs of students, educators and the institutions that serve them.

  • Custom curricula and lesson plans
  • Student workbooks and instructional resources
  • Facilitator and educator guides
  • Experiential learning and assessments
02

Educational Product & Program Development

Having a product, technology or specialized body of knowledge is not the same as having a curriculum. We work with clients and their subject matter experts to turn their content into age-appropriate instruction.

  • Curricula built around proprietary products and content
  • Technical and specialized content adaptation
  • Educational program design and implementation planning
  • Instructor preparation and train-the-trainer resources
  • Respect for client confidentiality and intellectual property
03

Student Academies & Enrichment Programs

Give students opportunities to explore ideas, practice new skills and apply what they learn through purposeful activities and projects.

  • Summer and after-school programs
  • Youth leadership and entrepreneurship
  • College and career readiness
  • Innovation and problem-solving
  • Domestic and international program partnerships
04

Educator & School Leadership Development

Support the adults responsible for creating effective learning environments, managing teams and carrying out school and district priorities.

  • School and district leadership
  • Management and staff development
  • School culture and educator retention
  • Strategic planning and change management
Summer Academies & Educational Partnerships

Give students a summer of discovery.

TTI is inviting schools, universities, nonprofits and program sponsors to explore week-long student academies. Programs can be designed around student age, institutional goals and available instructional time.

Students leave with practical tools, completed activities or projects they can use beyond the academy.

For school and district personnel

Invest in the people who make schools work.

Explore professional learning and advisory services available on-site or virtually, as workshops, cohorts or customized engagements.

School and District Leadership Development

Develop principals, assistant principals, central office leaders, and emerging administrators through practical learning in decision-making, communication, team leadership, change, and execution. Available as workshops or a multi-session leadership cohort.

Teacher Retention and School Culture Strategy

Help district leaders examine the workplace conditions, management practices, communication, and support systems that influence educator experience and retention, then identify practical improvement priorities.

Supervisory and Management Skills for School Leaders

Equip principals, department leaders, central office managers, and supervisors with practical approaches to delegation, feedback, coaching, accountability, conflict resolution, and team management.

Change Management for School Systems

Support district initiatives involving new systems, changing priorities, reorganizations, or technology adoption through stakeholder engagement, communication, implementation planning, and manager preparation.

Service Excellence and Communication for School Staff

Help front-office, administrative, and family-facing personnel communicate effectively, handle difficult conversations, respond to concerns, and deliver consistent service to students and families.

New Educator Onboarding and Mentorship Design

Support the design of structured onboarding and mentorship processes, including role expectations, mentor preparation, communication, and feedback practices for new educators and staff.

Strategic Planning Facilitation

Facilitate planning with district leaders and stakeholders to define priorities, establish measurable goals, identify responsibilities, and connect strategic plans to implementation and progress reviews.

Custom Workshops, Cohorts, and Self-Paced Modules

Adapt courses and programs to district needs through workshops, leadership cohorts, facilitated working sessions, and custom learning resources. Self-paced options may be developed by agreement.
